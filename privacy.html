Privacy policy

OpenClaw Agent is a private tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own Google account.

  • It accesses Google user data only to perform tasks the owner requests.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.
  • No data from any other person's Google account is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
